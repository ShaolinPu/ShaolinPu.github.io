***Thanks for visiting!***


I am a Ph.D. candidate in Analytics, Information and Operations, specializing in Information Systems, at the University of Kansas School of Business. 

My research examines how digital platforms and emerging technologies shape entrepreneurial strategies and market outcomes. I combine econometric methods with text analysis and artificial intelligence to study strategic decision-making in digital markets. My dissertation focuses on reward-based crowdfunding, exploring how creators communicate with backers, respond to blockbuster projects, and time their launches in competitive environments. More broadly, I study platform operations, online text evaluation, technology-enabled business decisions, and the role of AI agents in digital markets. I use econometrics and causal inference, NLP and large language models, and analytical modeling to address these questions.

My teaching interests include business analytics, operations and supply chain management, information systems, and AI applications in business. At KU, I have taught Data Analysis and Forecasting and received the 2026 John O. Tollefson Doctoral Student Teaching Award. I also developed the BSAN Model Building Workbench, an open-source tool supporting structured human–AI collaboration in analytics education.

Outside academia, I enjoy running, bouldering, hiking, cooking, and being with my family.

If you are interested in discussing about research and teaching, feel free to email me at shaolin@ku.edu or connect with me on LinkedIn.
